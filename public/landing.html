FAQ

Questions schools usually ask

How long does setup take?

Most schools are running within a few days. Classes, fee structure and the student list can all be added in bulk, and we help with the first import.

Is our data safe?

Every user has their own login and role, so teachers, accountants and parents only see what they are allowed to. Each school's data is kept separate, every address uses secure HTTPS, passwords are encrypted, and anyone can turn on two-factor login with an authenticator app. You can export your data at any time.

Do we get our own website?

Yes. Every school gets a website at its own address, for example yourschool.aasschoolmanager.com. You change the text, colours, photos, principal's message and gallery from the admin panel, and notices and events appear on it automatically.

We have more than one campus. Can the owner see all of them?

Yes. Each campus works on its own with its own admin and data, and the owner gets one login that shows all campuses together and can open any campus.

Can parents and teachers use it on their phones?

Yes. The portal works in any mobile browser, so parents and teachers do not need to install anything.

Can we send SMS and WhatsApp messages?

Yes. Absence alerts, fee reminders and general notices can go out by SMS, WhatsApp or both, once your SMS / WhatsApp account is connected.

Can parents pay fees online?

Parents see your bank, JazzCash or Easypaisa account in their portal, pay, and upload the proof. You approve it and the receipt is issued automatically.

Can we export our data?

Yes. Student lists, fee invoices, defaulters, attendance and results export to Excel (CSV), and vouchers, receipts, report cards and salary slips are PDFs.
